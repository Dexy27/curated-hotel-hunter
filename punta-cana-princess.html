Hotel Finds / Dominican Republic

Firsthand stay · Bávaro, Dominican Republic

Punta Cana Princess

A small, secluded all-inclusive on a beautiful stretch of white sand.

Highlights
  • White-sand beach with walks in both directions
  • No queues at meals during our stay
  • Excellent rum cocktails
See photos from my stay

What it was like to stay here

The beach was my favourite part of the stay. The sand was beautifully white, and we could walk along the shore in either direction beyond the resort. There was seaweed in the water when we visited in late November 2022.

The resort felt small and secluded, and we never had to queue for a meal. The food was just okay. The cocktails were excellent, especially the rum ones, of course.
