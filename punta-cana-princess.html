Firsthand stay · Bávaro, Dominican Republic

Punta Cana Princess

A small, secluded all-inclusive on a beautiful stretch of white sand.

Highlights
  • White-sand beach with walks in both directions
  • No queues at meals during our stay
  • Excellent rum cocktails
See photos from my stay

What it was like to stay here

The white-sand beach was my favourite part, with walks beyond the resort in both directions.

We saw seaweed in the water in late November 2022.

The resort felt small and secluded, and we never queued for meals.

The food was just okay.

The rum cocktails were excellent.
