What early May was like for us

We were at Vilamendhoo over 1 May 2024 and had rain on just one day of our week-long stay. We had much wetter weather on a different Maldives island in late November 2018.

May can bring lower resort rates, and temperatures stay warm. The southwest monsoon typically begins around mid-May, and rain and sea conditions can vary by island and week.

Barefoot dining at Ahima

Both villas were assigned to Ahima, the separate adults-only buffet restaurant by the infinity pool. We loved how relaxed it felt. The restaurant had a sand floor, so we could walk in barefoot.

Before you book

Vilamendhoo is in the South Ari Atoll, about 25 minutes by seaplane from Velana International Airport. A coral house reef wraps around the island, making Vilamendhoo an excellent choice for snorkeling straight from shore. If you’re comparing rates, check whether the seaplane transfer and your preferred meal plan are included in the total.

The resort announced a 15 November 2026 reopening following renovations. Check the resort’s current villa listings for layouts and amenities.
