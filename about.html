About us

Make memories for less.

Curated Hotel Hunter is an independent travel-curation concept for people who appreciate beautiful hotels and smart spending.

Our goal is simple: identify stays that combine the experience you want with a price that makes sense. Each find is selected for a reason, with transparent details and a considered editorial point of view.

CHH publishes firsthand hotel experiences and original photography alongside researched discoveries. We distinguish hotels we have personally visited from those selected through research. Explore our Vilamendhoo, Ao Prao and Riu Jambo features.

Learn how we select hotels or contact us at hello@curatedhotelhunter.com.
