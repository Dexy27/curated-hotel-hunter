Hotel Finds / Zanzibar

First-hand stay · February 2024

Riu Jambo Zanzibar

I stayed at Riu Jambo in February 2024. These photos take you from the palm-framed beach and bright turquoise shoreline to the pool and the overwater walkway at dusk.

During my February stay, I saw very little difference between low and high tide and could swim throughout the day. The food was another highlight. I’ve stayed at many all-inclusive resorts, and Riu Jambo had the best food I’ve had at any of them.
