Curaçao in August at Avila Beach Hotel

Curaçao Avila Beach Hotel · Willemstad, Curaçao

Why visit in August?

August falls in Curaçao’s typically dry season, and the island sits south of the main Atlantic hurricane belt. Direct hurricane hits are rare, though passing storms can still affect local conditions.
