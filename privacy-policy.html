Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Curated Hotel Hunter (CHH), operated by Deana Demjen Jankulov in Spain, respects your privacy. For privacy enquiries, contact curatedhotelhunter@gmail.com.

Information we process

At present, this website does not provide account registration or contact forms. If you email us, we process the information you provide, including your email address and message, to respond to your enquiry and manage correspondence.

Purpose and legal basis

We use correspondence data to answer requests and communicate with you, relying on our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries or, where applicable, taking steps requested before entering into an agreement. We do not sell your personal information.

Hosting and technical data

The website is hosted using Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details and security logs to deliver and protect the website, under its own applicable terms and privacy documentation. Processing may involve transfers outside the European Economic Area subject to applicable safeguards.

Cookies and analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand where visits come from, which pages people visit and which hotel details they open and which hotel links they click. Google receives information about the device and browser, visits and clicks. We use this information to improve the website and choose which hotels to feature. Google Analytics advertising features are disabled.

The optional _ga and _ga_1MRJFJEYK4 cookies distinguish browsers and maintain session information. They are set for up to 180 days. The chh_analytics cookie remembers your choice for 180 days. Use the Cookie settings button in the footer to change your choice or withdraw consent at any time.

Google processes analytics data on our behalf. This may involve transfers outside the European Economic Area under applicable safeguards. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Affiliate links and external websites

We identify affiliate links on offer cards. When you follow an external link, the affiliate network and booking provider may use their own tracking, subject to their privacy policies.

Retention and recipients

We retain email correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain records and meet applicable legal obligations. Our email and hosting providers may process data on our behalf or as independent controllers, as applicable.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact us at curatedhotelhunter@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) at www.aepd.es.

Updates

We may revise this notice as website features and data practices change. The latest version will be posted here with an updated date.
