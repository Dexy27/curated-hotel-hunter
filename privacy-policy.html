Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Curated Hotel Hunter (CHH), operated by Deana Demjen Jankulov in Spain, respects your privacy. For privacy enquiries, contact curatedhotelhunter@gmail.com.

At present, this website does not provide account registration or contact forms. If you email us, we process the information you provide, including your email address and message, to respond to your enquiry and manage correspondence.

The website is hosted using Cloudflare. Processing may involve transfers outside the European Economic Area subject to applicable safeguards.

We have not intentionally installed analytics, advertising or affiliate tracking scripts in this version of the website. Essential hosting and security technologies may still be used by our hosting provider. If we introduce optional analytics or marketing cookies, we will update this notice and implement consent controls where required.

Affiliate links and external websites

We do not currently operate affiliate links on this website. If we add affiliate partnerships, we will identify them clearly and update our privacy and cookie information as needed. Links to external websites are governed by those websites' own privacy policies.

Retention and recipients

We retain email correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain records and meet applicable legal obligations. Our email and hosting providers may process data on our behalf or as independent controllers, as applicable.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact us at curatedhotelhunter@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) at www.aepd.es.

Updates

We may revise this notice as website features and data practices change. The latest version will be posted here with an updated date.
